Privacy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

This website is small on purpose. It sets no cookies and uses no analytics or tracking. Personal data only reaches us when you write to us.

Who is responsible

Kratos Space, the KRATOS team, a student team at ETH Zurich, Zürich, Switzerland.
Contact: info@kratos-space.ch

What we collect and why

  • Contact form: your name, email address and message. We use them only to reply to you and to follow up on your request.
  • Visiting the site: to deliver the pages, the hosting provider processes technical data such as your IP address, browser and the time of your visit. We do not see or use this data.
  • Fonts: the fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, so your browser sends your IP address to Google when the page loads.

Who processes it

  • Formspree, Inc. (USA) receives messages from the contact form, keeps them in our Formspree account and forwards them to our inbox by email. Formspree privacy policy
  • GitHub, Inc. (USA) hosts this website through GitHub Pages. GitHub privacy statement
  • Google (USA) serves the fonts through Google Fonts. Google Fonts privacy FAQ
  • Our email provider, Swizzonic, hosts the inbox at info@kratos-space.ch where your message arrives.

Formspree, GitHub and Google may process data in the USA. When your data goes there, it is protected by the safeguards these providers describe in their privacy policies.

How long we keep it

We keep your message only as long as we need it to answer you and follow up. After that we delete it from our inbox and from our Formspree account.

Your rights

You can ask us what data we have about you, and ask us to correct or delete it. Just write to info@kratos-space.ch. You can also contact the Swiss Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC).
